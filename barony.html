The Baronies of the Principality of Oertha

Created June 26, XXXVII (2002)
Updated January 5, LXI (2026)


The Arms of Oertha
Azure, a wolf sejant, head erect,
in chief two compass stars, and on a base Argent,
a laurel wreath Azure

&

&
